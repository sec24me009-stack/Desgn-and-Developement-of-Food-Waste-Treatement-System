# VibraSep — Test Plan

## 1. Objective

Define repeatable tests to evaluate the operation, performance, safety, and maintainability of the proposed kitchen waste segregation and liquid treatment system.

## 2. Test Status

All tests below are planned tests. Results must be recorded after simulation or physical prototype testing. No test is considered passed until evidence is documented.

## 3. Test Cases

| Test ID | Test | Method | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| T01 | Input volume | Measure slurry volume before feeding | Recorded input volume matches the test plan |
| T02 | Feed flow rate | Measure volume delivered over time | Actual rate is recorded and compared with the target |
| T03 | Mesh sequence | Inspect screen order | 10 mm top, 8 mm middle, 2 mm bottom |
| T04 | Solid retention | Weigh retained solids and measure suspended solids in liquid | Record retention and suspended-solids reduction |
| T05 | Liquid recovery | Measure collected liquid volume | Record recovery percentage and losses |
| T06 | Grease removal | Measure oil and grease before and after the trap | Calculate removal efficiency using measured concentrations |
| T07 | Carbon filtration | Compare selected water-quality parameters before and after filtration | Record changes; do not assume the output is potable |
| T08 | Clogging | Operate the system with representative waste | Record blockage, flow reduction, and cleaning time |
| T09 | Leakage | Inspect joints and tanks during operation | No visible leakage during the defined test |
| T10 | Cleaning and maintenance | Time the cleaning and component removal process | Record time and maintenance difficulties |

## 4. Calculations

### Flow rate

Flow rate = Collected or fed volume / Elapsed time

### Liquid recovery

Liquid recovery (%) = (Collected liquid volume / Input liquid volume) × 100

### Oil and grease removal

Removal (%) = [(Inlet concentration − Outlet concentration) / Inlet concentration] × 100

### Suspended-solids reduction

Reduction (%) = [(Inlet concentration − Outlet concentration) / Inlet concentration] × 100

## 5. Safety

Use suitable protective equipment when handling food waste and wastewater. Clean the equipment after testing, prevent contact with moving parts, and dispose of waste according to applicable requirements.

## 6. Reporting

For every test, record the date, test ID, input composition, operating conditions, raw measurements, calculations, deviations, photographs where appropriate, and pass/fail status.

Do not report proposed targets as achieved results. Use measured experimental data to support performance claims.
